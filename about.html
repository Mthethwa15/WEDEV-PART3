About Sinendaba Charity Centre

Serving Tembisa and Gauteng with warmth, compassion, and dignity since 2015.

Our History

Sinendaba Charity Center is all about providing for the less privileged community. It is based in Tembisa and famously known for their generosity and warmth towards both homeless children and adults.

It was founded by Gomolemo Keetse and Ntando Motau in 2015. Not only does it cater for the homeless but also those that don’t have the means to nutritiously provide for their households.

Our Mission

To serve, protect and support children and adults by providing shelter, warm beds, nutritious meals, hygiene, and other basic needs for a better and brighter future.

Our Vision

A center that offers a better world for the community with love and warmth.
